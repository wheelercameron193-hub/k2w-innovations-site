Who We Are
Built to Bridge Finance & Technology

K.2.W Innovations Ltd is a financial technology consulting and development firm incorporated in the state of Wyoming, with its principal office in Riverside, California.

Founded by Kijan Cameron Wheeler, the company was established to close the gap between legacy financial systems and modern software — delivering the integrations, infrastructure, and strategic guidance that businesses need to operate in a digitally-native financial ecosystem.

We work across the full fintech stack: from regulatory strategy and API architecture to production deployment and ongoing maintenance. Our clients range from early-stage startups building their first financial product to established businesses modernizing their payment and data infrastructure.
